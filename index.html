Trace Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

1. Information We Collect

  • Content you add (logs and entries, including titles, text, timestamps, icons/colors).
  • Location data if you enable location features (e.g., map view).
  • Basic diagnostics/crash data to improve app reliability.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide core app features (create, view, and manage logs/entries).
  • To show location-based features if enabled.
  • To maintain and improve the app (diagnostics/crash data).

3. Data Storage and Deletion

  • Your data is stored on your device. You can delete logs and entries at any time within the app.
  • If you uninstall the app, on‑device data is typically removed. Data in system backups may persist per your device settings.

4. Sharing

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We may share limited data with service providers (e.g., diagnostics/crash reporting) to run and improve the app.
  • We may disclose information if required by law or to protect safety, rights, or property.

5. Your Choices

  • Control location permissions in device settings.
  • Create, edit, and delete your data within the app.
  • Contact us to request access or deletion where applicable by law.

6. Security

  • We use reasonable measures to protect your data, but no method is 100% secure.

7. Changes

  • We may update this policy and will revise the “Last updated” date. Continued use constitutes acceptance.
