Trace Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

1. Information We Collect

  • Content you add: Logs and entries, including titles, text, timestamps, photos, ratings, and icons/colors.
  • Location data: Location data if you enable location features (e.g., map view).
  • Basic diagnostics: Crash data to improve app reliability.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide core app features (create, view, and manage logs/entries).
  • To show location-based features if enabled.
  • To maintain and improve the app (diagnostics/crash data).

3. Data Storage and Deletion

  • Your data is stored locally on your device and is securely synced to your personal Apple iCloud account (via CloudKit).
  • You can delete logs and entries at any time within the app.
  • If you uninstall the app, on‑device data is typically removed. Data securely synced to iCloud or in system backups may persist per your device and Apple account settings.

4. Sharing

  • We do not sell your data.
  • No Third-Party Trackers: We do not integrate third-party analytics or tracking services. Basic crash and diagnostic data is only shared with Apple if you have opted into sharing analytics with developers in your device settings.
  • We may disclose information if required by law or to protect safety, rights, or property.

5. Your Choices

  • Control location permissions in your device settings.
  • Create, edit, and delete your data directly within the app.
  • Manage your iCloud storage through your Apple ID settings.
  • Contact us to request access or deletion where applicable by law.

6. Security

  • We use reasonable measures to protect your data, relying on Apple's secure ecosystem and CloudKit infrastructure, but no method of digital storage is 100% secure.

7. Changes

  • We may update this policy and will revise the "Last updated" date. Continued use constitutes acceptance.
