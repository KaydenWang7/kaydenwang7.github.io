01 — Logs

Create logs for every event

Airshows, breakfast spots, a week in Taipei. Each log gets its own icon and color, so your collections stay distinct — and easy to open again.

  • Swipe left on a log to add an entry instantly
  • Search any log when the list gets long
  • Photo gallery and map views per log

02 — Entries

Add detailed data

An entry is more than a title. Timestamp, rating to two decimals, photos, a map pin, and a note — the full texture of the moment, kept in one place.

  • Optional location recording
  • Rating gradients that glow with the score
  • Camera capture, right from the entry

03 — Statistics

Review your statistics

Average, highest, lowest. A chart of how your ratings land, and how often you log — by day, month, or year. See the rhythm of a habit instead of scrolling for it.

  • Rating distribution at a glance
  • Frequency charts across any time scale
  • Full stats and a globe map with Trace Pro

04 — Maps

Map out your journey

Drop every pin onto an interactive map. Filter by log, zoom into a neighborhood, and retrace a trip by where you actually stood.

  • All-entries map across your logs
  • North-locked main map for a steady read
  • Tap a pin to open its entry
